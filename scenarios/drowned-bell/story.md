## Premise
The fishing village of Saltmere is being emptied boat by boat while a cult called the Tide-Whisperers prepares, atop the old lighthouse, to wake the ancient sea entity bound beneath the coast.

## Main Quest
Stop the Tide-Whisperers from completing their ritual atop the lighthouse before the Fathomless Choir wakes and drags Saltmere into the sea.

## Active Quests

## DM Plan
- The party arrives at Saltmere Docks to frightened, tight-lipped villagers, missing boats, and rumors of chanting carried on the tide.
- Investigating the Village Square and Chapel Ruins reveals the old shrine has been claimed by a cult, with signs pointing up the cliff path toward the lighthouse.
- A lead from Old Corwin in the Smugglers' Den (or the party's own exploring) opens a route through the Tide Caves, where a mutated guardian and cult chanting point deeper toward a Drowned Shrine.
- In the Drowned Shrine, the party can claim the Sunken Reliquary from the Grimtide Warden guarding it — an artifact rumored able to bind the Fathomless Choir back to sleep without further bloodshed.
- At the Lighthouse Sanctum, High Priest Maren Voss nears completion of the ritual; the party must defeat her in combat or use the Sunken Reliquary to unravel the ritual before the Choir wakes.

## Story So Far
- The party has just arrived at Saltmere's docks, drawn by rumors of vanished boats and a village gripped by fear.

## Win Conditions
- Defeat High Priest Maren Voss at the Lighthouse Sanctum before the ritual completes.
- Alternatively, retrieve the Sunken Reliquary from the Drowned Shrine and use it at the Lighthouse Sanctum to unravel the ritual without killing Voss.

## Opening Narration
Welcome, adventurers. Salt spray stings your eyes as you step onto the rotting boards of Saltmere's docks, where half the fishing boats sit unmanned and nets hang forgotten in the wind. The few villagers who meet your eyes speak only in murmurs, of neighbors gone in the night, of chanting carried on the tide, of lights burning atop the old lighthouse where none have burned in years. Somewhere in this fog-choked village an answer waits, and something ancient stirs beneath the waves whether you find it in time or not. What do you do first?
