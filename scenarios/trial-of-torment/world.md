title: The Trial of Torment
---
## Setting
The Free City of Skarn is built atop a dungeon older than its own foundations — a warren of pits, poison darts, riddle-locks, and worse, said to have been carved by an order of trapsmiths who vanished centuries ago. Once a year, Baron Threnody throws open its iron gate for the Trial of Torment: any contestant who reaches the vault at its heart and claims the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes keeps it, and their life besides. Most don't manage either. The nobles of Skarn treat it as the social event of the season, wagering fortunes on how far this year's fools will get before the dungeon claims them, and the Baron never lacks for contestants — desperation, debt, and glory keep the entrance hall full every year.

## Factions
- Baron Threnody's Court: The nobles who fund the Trial and watch it for sport, profit, and the wagers it generates.
- Rival Contestants: Other desperate souls entering the dungeon this year for the same prize — not necessarily allies.
- The Silent Architects: The long-vanished order of trapsmiths who built the dungeon and left the Warden of Torment as its final guardian.
