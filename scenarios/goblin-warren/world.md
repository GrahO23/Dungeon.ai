title: The Goblin Warren
---
## Setting
The farming village of Millbrook sits at the edge of the Greywood, a stretch of old forest that
folk have learned to leave alone after dark. A tribe of goblins has lived undisturbed in the
warren beneath the wood's roots for as long as anyone can remember — until three nights ago, when
a raiding party slipped into Millbrook and made off with the Sunstone, a fist-sized relic that has
kept the village's wells from freezing over every winter for three generations. Without it, the
first hard frost will be a disaster.

## Factions
- Millbrook Villagers: Frightened, practical, and out of options — they can offer supplies and
  gratitude, but no fighters of their own.
- The Warren Tribe: Goblins under Chieftain Grulka, who see the Sunstone as a trophy and a
  bargaining chip rather than something they understand the true worth of.
