title: The Drowned Bell
---
## Setting
Saltmere is a fishing village clinging to a storm-battered stretch of coast, its wharves rotting and its nets going unmended as more boats fail to return each week. The fog here never fully lifts, and on the worst nights the villagers swear they hear a bell tolling from somewhere beneath the waves, though no bell has hung in the old chapel tower for a generation. Beneath the fear and the salt-rot lies something older than the village itself: a slumbering thing the sea has never quite let go of, and a handful of the desperate and the devout who have decided it's time to wake it.

## Factions
- The Tide-Whisperers: A secretive cult of drowned sailors' descendants and desperate villagers who believe waking the Fathomless Choir will end Saltmere's decline rather than doom it.
- Saltmere Fisherfolk: The dwindling, frightened townsfolk who just want their missing kin found and the disappearances to stop.
- The Fathomless Choir: An ancient, slumbering sea entity bound beneath the coast generations ago, which the cult's ritual is meant to wake.
