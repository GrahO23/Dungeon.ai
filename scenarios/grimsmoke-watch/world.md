## Setting
Grimsmoke is a sprawling, soot-stained port city where the great trade guilds hold as much power as the Council of Aldermen ever did, and where everything from thievery to assassination is a licensed, taxed, and jealously regulated profession — properly filed in triplicate. The City Watch is chronically underfunded, comically undermanned, and staffed by an assortment of the unpromotable, the unkillable, and the occasionally brilliant, and yet, against all reasonable odds, it usually manages to keep the whole ramshackle city from sliding into the canal. Rain falls on Grimsmoke roughly eleven months of the year, and the clock on the Watch House has struck the wrong hour since before anyone currently serving was born.

## Factions
- The Grimsmoke City Watch: Underfunded, overworked, and staffed by an unlikely assortment of misfits who somehow keep the peace anyway.
- The Assassins' Guild: A meticulously bureaucratic institution that treats murder as a licensed, closely regulated trade, and takes a very dim view of anyone breaking its rules.
- The Council of Aldermen: The city's guild-appointed rulers, considerably more concerned with tax revenue and appearances than with a body in the canal.
