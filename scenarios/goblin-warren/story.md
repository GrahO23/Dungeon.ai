## Premise
The goblin tribe of the Greywood has stolen Millbrook's Sunstone, and the village will not
survive the coming frost without it.

## Main Quest
Recover the Sunstone from Chieftain Grulka in the depths of the goblin warren and return it to
Millbrook.

## Active Quests

## DM Plan
- The party sets out from Millbrook and tracks the raiders to the Warren Gate, where a sentry
  challenges them.
- Inside, the tunnels split: the Goblin Den holds Skrit, a scout who can be fought, bribed, or
  talked into revealing the chieftain's guard rotation.
- The Tunnel Junction is the last calm ground before the Chieftain's Hall — the party can rest
  and plan here.
- In the Chieftain's Hall, Grulka will not give up the Sunstone without a fight, unless the party
  has already turned Skrit or found another way to undermine her authority first.
- With the Sunstone recovered, the party returns to Millbrook before the first frost.

## Story So Far
- Three nights ago, goblin raiders slipped into Millbrook and stole the Sunstone; the party has
  set out at first light to track them into the Greywood.

## Win Conditions
- Defeat Chieftain Grulka in the Chieftain's Hall and take back the Sunstone.
- Alternatively, successfully persuade or intimidate Grulka into surrendering the Sunstone
  peacefully (a hard persuasion or intimidation check), avoiding the fight entirely.

## Opening Narration
Welcome, adventurers. Millbrook sent you into the Greywood at first light, following a trail of
broken branches and goblin tracks toward the hill where the raiders vanished. The forest closes in
around you, the village already out of sight behind. Ahead, the path narrows toward a hillside you
can't yet see — but the tracks are fresh, and the frost is coming. What do you do?
