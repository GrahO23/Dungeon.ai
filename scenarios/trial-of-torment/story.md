## Premise
Once a year, Baron Threnody opens the ancient deathtrap dungeon beneath Skarn for the Trial of Torment: reach the vault at its heart and claim the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes, or become one more set of bones added to its collection.

## Main Quest
Survive the deathtrap dungeon's traps, hazards, and guardians to reach the Vault Chamber and claim the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes.

## Active Quests

## DM Plan
- The party enters as contestants in the Entrance Antechamber, hearing the Trial's rules read out before the iron gate seals shut behind them.
- The Trapped Hall splits the route ahead: the brute hazards of the Pit Gallery and Sunken Armory, or the puzzle route through the Riddle Door and Mirror Maze — either is passable.
- In the Sunken Armory, past the Pit Wretch and the Drowned Champion, the party can recover the Brass Sigil among the drowned gear.
- At the Riddle Door, the Keeper of Riddles offers safe passage through the Mirror Maze to anyone who solves its riddle rather than fighting.
- Both routes converge at the Vault Chamber, where the Warden of Torment guards the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes — to be defeated in combat, or bypassed by using the Brass Sigil to disarm it.

## Story So Far
- The party has just entered the Entrance Antechamber as contestants in this year's Trial of Torment, the iron gate sealing shut behind them.

## Win Conditions
- Defeat the Warden of Torment in the Vault Chamber and claim the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes.
- Alternatively, use the Brass Sigil (found in the Sunken Armory) at the Vault Chamber to disarm the Warden of Torment and claim the Jewel without a fight.

## Opening Narration
Welcome, adventurers — or should I say, contestants. Torches gutter along the stone walls of Baron Threnody's antechamber as his herald reads out the same rules he reads every year: enter the Trial of Torment, survive the dungeon below, and whoever reaches the vault and claims the Jewel of a Thousand Eyes keeps it, along with their life. Behind you, the iron gate is already grinding shut, and it does not open again from this side. Ahead, a single passage leads down into torchlit dark, and from what little you can hear, something down there is looking forward to meeting you. What do you do?
