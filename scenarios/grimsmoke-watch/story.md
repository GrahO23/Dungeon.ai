## Premise
A body pulled from Rattlebridge Canal is clutching a guild seal that, by every official record, was never issued to anyone — and the chronically underfunded Grimsmoke City Watch has to work out why before whoever forged it kills again.

## Main Quest
Trace the forged guild seal to whoever is behind it and put a stop to the unlicensed operation running beneath the city.

## Active Quests

## DM Plan
- The party, members of the Grimsmoke Watch, are called to Rattlebridge Canal to examine a body clutching a guild seal that shouldn't exist.
- Following the seal into the Tannery District and then the Guild Quarter, the party learns it's a forgery — someone is running unlicensed, guild-code-breaking contracts, a scandal the legitimate Assassins' Guild wants quietly resolved.
- The trail splits: High Street and the Aldermen's Counting-House lead toward who's funding the operation, while the Thieves' Market leads toward where the forged contracts are actually being fenced.
- Both threads converge on the Sewer Tunnels, where "the Underwriter" — the rogue guild clerk behind it all — has set up an unlicensed contract-brokerage.
- The party must corner the Underwriter directly, or assemble enough evidence (the forged seal, the counting-house ledger, and the Guildmistress's cooperation) to have the Guild itself shut the operation down without further bloodshed.

## Story So Far
- The party, members of the Grimsmoke Watch, has just been called to Rattlebridge Canal, where a body was pulled from the water clutching a guild seal that by all rights shouldn't exist.

## Win Conditions
- Defeat the Underwriter in the Sewer Tunnels hideout beneath the city.
- Alternatively, gather the forged guild seal, the Ledger of Forged Contracts (from the Aldermen's Counting-House), and Guildmistress Blackledger's cooperation, then bring the case to the Guild Quarter to have the Guild deal with the Underwriter internally.

## Opening Narration
Welcome, adventurers, or more accurately, welcome to another gloriously thankless shift in the Grimsmoke City Watch. Rain needles down over Rattlebridge Canal, where a constable stands guard over a body just fished from the water, one hand still closed around a small brass seal that, according to every ledger in the city, was never issued to anyone. Somewhere behind you, the Watch House clock is striking an hour it already struck twenty minutes ago, which nobody has ever gotten around to fixing. Whatever this seal really is, somebody wanted it in that canal badly enough to put a man in after it. What do you do first?
